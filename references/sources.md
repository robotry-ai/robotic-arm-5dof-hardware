# Sources and datasheets

Use manufacturer documentation as the primary authority. Seller listings are useful for availability and price, not for resolving conflicting technical specifications.

## Manufacturer documentation starting points
- Analog Devices / Trinamic TMC2209: https://www.analog.com/en/products/tmc2209.html
- ams OSRAM AS5600 product documentation: https://ams-osram.com/products/sensor-solutions/position-sensors/ams-as5600-position-sensor
- Infineon / ams OSRAM magnetic position sensor documentation: https://www.infineon.com/
- TowerPro MG996R product documentation: https://www.towerpro.com.tw/
- Waveshare SC15 product page: https://www.waveshare.com/sc15-servo.htm
- Waveshare SC15 wiki: https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/SC15_Servo

## Research record required for every purchase candidate
- Exact manufacturer and full part number.
- Datasheet URL and revision/date.
- Supply voltage, continuous/peak current and thermal limits.
- Torque-speed curve, not only stall torque.
- Dimensions, mounting, mass and connector pinout.
- India seller URL, price, shipping and date checked.
- Clear note if the item is a clone/compatible product rather than genuine.

## Caveats
- NEMA 17 is a frame size, not a motor model or torque rating.
- MG996R listings may refer to clones with different performance.
- Smart-servo specifications are model/revision-specific; verify bus voltage, protocol, travel and current.
- Sensor resolution is not system accuracy. Magnet alignment, eccentricity, mounting stiffness, calibration and backlash all matter.

Prefer linking to official datasheets. Only commit datasheet copies if redistribution is permitted.
