## BOM categories
**Mechanical:** printed links/housings, bearings, shafts, spacers, fasteners, horns/couplers, encoder magnets/brackets, cable management and end-effector mount.

**Electronics:** ESP32 board, five joint actuators, drivers or bus interface, encoders where needed, motor supply, logic regulator, distribution, fuses, emergency disconnect, wiring and connectors.

**Test equipment:** multimeter, suitable peak-current measurement, current-limited bench supply where possible, calipers and weighing scale.
